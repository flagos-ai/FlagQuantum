# Retained native MCX program

All50 controls, one target, all-zero preparation and static helper composition
are preserved. The compiler reuses indexed control-X effects and bounded clean
scratch. Runtime proves rank one from the submitted closed I/X/Z/CCX
sequence and raw CircuitIR zero initialization before selecting MPS. The sealed
plan and native result are verified again at execution/readout. Exact product
sites preserve the complete state and absolute sign, including13 scratch bits;
no dense full-state allocation is made. Active, near-active and nonzero target
inputs are covered separately. This is local correctness evidence, not a
scalability or complete Notebook-workflow claim.
