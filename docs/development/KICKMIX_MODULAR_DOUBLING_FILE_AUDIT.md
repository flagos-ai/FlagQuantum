## Width-256 benchmarks

Both workloads use one quantum enable, tolerance 30, four clean work wires
and 256 borrowed dirty wires. The constant workload uses the locked prime
ending in `FFFFFC2F`. The mixed workload uses 254 classical input bits
with literal ones at the modulus endpoints. The tagged physical transport
also declares allocator scratch which starts in zero. The constant layout
uses scratch slots `b2` through `b60`. The mixed layout uses `b256`
through `b507` in addition to its caller-provided modulus slots.
The remaining feedback slots are defined by emitted writes.

| Workload | Operations and scopes | Marks | Total records | R | HMR | CCX | CSWAP | Source generation | Native generation |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Constant modulus | 1,170 | 16 | 1,186 | 63 | 63 | 186 | 255 | 7.9 μs | 10.622 ms |
| Mixed modulus | 7,011 | 24 | 7,035 | 265 | 266 | 825 | 255 | 43 μs | 68.593 ms |

Counts are emitted physical records. Register directives are excluded on
the source side and the native transport header is excluded. The serialized
body is otherwise identical. No runtime expansion or fault-tolerance
conversion is included. Native timings are medians of seven generator
runs after one warmup, measured while local checks ran. Both timings
exclude operand layout construction. The native path also validates the
physical artifact.

A separate native Simulator check used 64 deterministic width-256 basis
inputs per workload on each implementation. Every sampled target matched
the ideal controlled doubling result, phase was zero, control and modulus
inputs were retained, borrowed dirty work was restored, and all four
clean wires returned to zero. The exhaustive quantum branch evidence
remains scoped to the smaller widths in the prior acceptance documents.

## Open boundary

Finite tolerance intentionally preserves the source approximation. The
locked modulus-13, tolerance-zero witness sends target seven to fourteen
where exact doubling would send it to one. The all-literal-ones fallback
can pass an empty complemented offset. The source inverse failed in the
recorded probe, and the native path rejects that domain. K-0121 remains
open for this boundary and its inherited mixed-adder limitations.
